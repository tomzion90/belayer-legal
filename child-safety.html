Child Safety Standards — Blayer

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · Effective: 14 September 2026

Blayer is developed by Tom Zion. Climbers aged 13 and over use it to find climbing partners.

What we prohibit

Child sexual abuse and exploitation of any kind is prohibited on Blayer: sexual content involving anyone under 18, grooming, and any attempt to use Blayer to arrange contact with a minor for a sexual purpose.

How to report

Every profile and every chat has Report and Block. A report also blocks that person from you. A person — not an automated system — reads every report, and reports involving someone under 18 are put at the front of the queue.

What we do

We remove the account and content involved, keep what the law requires us to keep, and report to the relevant authorities, including the Israel Police.
