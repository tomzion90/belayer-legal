Account & Data Deletion — Blayer

Last updated: 5 July 2026

Blayer (the climbing-partner app, listed on Google Play as B-Layer), operated by Tom Zion, Yoval Sharbi, Nadav Madlal, and Sharon Elalof, lets you delete your account and all associated data at any time. This page explains exactly how.

Delete your account from inside the app (recommended)

Deletion is built into the app and takes effect immediately:

  1. Open the Blayer app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab (bottom-right).
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm in the dialog. Your account and all associated data are then permanently deleted.

If you can't access the app

Email yutomshazion@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll verify your request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What data is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of your personal data, including:

  • Your account and login (email / Apple or Google sign-in identifier).
  • Your profile — name, age, photos, climbing disciplines, grade range, weight, years of experience, gear, preferred gyms/crags, and availability.
  • Your activity — session requests, interests, matches, in-app chat messages, partner ratings and safety flags you submitted, and any blocks or reports you made.
  • Your notification/device token.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is retained after deletion in the normal case: your data is erased from our live systems immediately when you delete in the app (or within 30 days for an email request). Any residual copies in our providers' encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. We may retain the minimum required to comply with a legal obligation or to resolve a safety report already raised against the account; anything kept for that reason is deleted once it is no longer needed.
